Payment Plans & Collections

Manage scheduled payments, overdue balances, and customer follow-ups inside Odoo.

Stop chasing installments across Excel sheets, WhatsApp threads, and scattered notes. Turn scheduled and delayed customer payments into a structured workflow — for education centers, clinics, gyms, real estate, agencies, maintenance, B2B, and any business that bills on a schedule. Built for Odoo Community, no Enterprise required.

Installment Plans

Build a payment plan per customer, generate the schedule from reusable templates (3 monthly, 50/50, quarterly…), and track paid, remaining and overdue amounts automatically.

Overdue & Collections

Per-line status (pending, due, partial, paid, overdue) with grace periods, days-overdue, a dedicated Overdue Collections view, and follow-up records for every call, email or meeting.

Generic by design

Never tied to one industry. A generic Source Document reference links a plan to a sales order, invoice, project, contract or any custom model — only the ones you have installed appear.

Lightweight core, optional add-ons

The core depends only on base and mail. Add invoicing & payment sync, aging reports, sales or messaging integrations as separate optional modules when you need them.

What's included

  • Payment Plans with templates, plan types and a responsible user
  • Auto-generated installment lines with equal or first-payment-% distribution
  • Partial payment tracking, remaining/overdue rollups and next-due date
  • Collection follow-ups (call / WhatsApp / email / meeting / note)
  • Aging-ready statuses, useful filters, and smart buttons
  • Two security profiles: Payment Plan User and Manager
  • Full chatter & scheduled activities on plans and follow-ups
